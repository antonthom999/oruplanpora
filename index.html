Oruplanpora
Welcome to Oruplanpora, a website that generates architectural floor plans for you.

Create, explore, and experiment with floor plans ideas - completely free.
It works in 3 steps:
1
Choose your rooms
2
Adjust the connections and click generate
3
Edit your plan, add stuff

Oruplanpora is free for everyone and supported by advertisements. If you find it useful, please consider turning off your ad blocker or allowing ads on this website. Ads help us keep the generator free and accessible to everyone.

Thank you for being here - and happy designing!
